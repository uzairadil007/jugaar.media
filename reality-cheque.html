The question inside the question

The 180+ video opportunity.

You said how this gets answered tells you a lot about how someone thinks. So — the thinking, not just the answer.

"Repurpose the Founders Club knowledge base into organic content — without cannibalising the paid product."

The brief, paraphrased
01

Teach the what. Sell the how.

Curriculum splits into two layers: concepts/mindset shifts (public-safe) and templates/walkthroughs/1:1 application (the paid layer). Only the first layer becomes organic — mapped straight onto the Mindset and Educational Lead Magnet types already in the calendar.

02

Concept clips, not course clips.

Nothing gets posted as "Lesson 7." Every repurposed idea is re-cut with a new hook, new framing, and zero course branding — so it reads as free-standing content, not a leaked module.

03

The curiosity ladder.

Each piece ends on an open loop only Founders Club closes — "here's the framework, the exact pricing table is inside." Free content becomes inbound to the paid product instead of a substitute for it.

Before scaling to 180+: one topic gets A/B'd as free content in week one and watched for a cannibalisation signal — branded search dipping, trial cancellations, refund upticks — before the rest of the library gets greenlit. Systems get tested at small scale before they get trusted at full volume.

Creative direction for paid ads

Answer

Organic is the testing lab. Whatever clears the bar for engagement in a given week gets flagged to Ads the same week — same hook, same visual language, re-cut to spec. Paid briefs start from what already worked, not from a blank page.

Answer
