Once the Ambassador Is Live

The daily ritual

Same face, same time, every day. This is the cadence that turns strangers into a community — it starts the moment the Ambassador is confirmed and runs on repeat for all six months.

Non-negotiable content rules
  • Every post ties back to a feeling — stress, overwhelm, anxiety — before it shows the product.
  • No caption without a hook line in the first six words. Assume sound is off.
  • At least 1 in 4 posts is pure education, zero direct sell.
  • Visual system stays locked to lime, black, and white — no off-brand colour grading.
  • Every case-study post ends with a save-prompt CTA: "save this for your next exam week."
